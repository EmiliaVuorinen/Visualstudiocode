Interesting Moments During The Trip

  • I walked around 20,000 steps almost every day, London is very walkable.
  • We used the underground a lot, and I learned that it's very crowded especially in the afternoon.
  • I was able to be in a red double decker bus for the first time.
  • The weather in London was cloudy and rainy for the most part of my trip.
  • I tried authentic English food for the first time, Fish and Chips!
